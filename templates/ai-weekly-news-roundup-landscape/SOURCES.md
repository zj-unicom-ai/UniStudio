# Episode research and media credits

The bundled `data/sample-episode.json` is invented layout data. It is not a factual news report and has no external reporting sources. Replace it before publication.

For each real episode, keep a dated copy of this ledger alongside the episode data. Link the source that directly supports each factual claim; prefer an official announcement, filing, paper, or first-party statement, then add independent reporting when needed. Record uncertainty and the exact date checked. A short source label in the video does not replace this full record.

## Media credits

No news photographs, narration, music, or sound effects are included. For every imported image or audio asset, record its creator/source, direct URL or creation method, license or permission, required attribution, and the episode segment where it appears. Do not use a page's preview image merely because the page is cited as a news source; confirm that the image itself is licensed for reuse.

Bundled fonts and GSAP are documented separately in [TOOLS.md](TOOLS.md) and `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES/`.
